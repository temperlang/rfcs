# Summary 25-Nov-2026

This document captures the current state of RFCs to help prioritize and move proposals towards conclusion.

For each RFC, we capture:

- What benefits from it?  Initiatives, people's personal goals, anything really.
- How complete is it?  Placeholder, needs ideas, ready for scrutiny, ready for implementation.  Placeholder means it has some initial thoughts but needs fleshing out into the proper format with major sections written.  Needs ideas means we've identified a problem but have not collected solutions and research relevant to the topic.  Ready for scrutiny means at least one solution has been fleshed out and we can move beyond the idea generation part of brainstorming to the filtering and refining phase.  Ready for implementation means parties have had a chance to scrutinize and react and there is no outstanding disagreement to the proposal.
- Unlocks is anyone's short statement of a benefit that would come from solving this.
- What is the team's priority: urgent, nice-to-have, meh, never.  If someone wants this soon, they should say so.  And they should say what it unlocks.
- Who is responsible?
- What is the next action item? What does it need to move forward?

The goal of the periodic review is to fill in the last three fields.
